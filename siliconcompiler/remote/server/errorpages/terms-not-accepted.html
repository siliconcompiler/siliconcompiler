terms-not-accepted

Terms not accepted

403 Forbidden https://siliconcompiler.com/server-errors/terms-not-accepted

There is a document you have not accepted, and it covers something this request needs.

This one you can fix yourself. The body carries decision_url.

What to do

  1. Open decision_url in a browser.
  2. Read the document. blocked_by is its identifier, and it is also listed in GET /v1/me.
  3. Accept it, then retry the original request unchanged.

You may also decline, and you may change your mind later. Declining is a recorded answer, not a failure state.

Two scopes, and they cost different things

terms_scope tells you which case you are in, and they are genuinely different situations.

terms_scopeWhat is blockedHow you will have noticed
service Everything — no submission of any kind. GET /v1/me reports can_submit: false, and blocked_reason is this type’s URI.
resource Only the jobs that name the covered resource. can_submit stays true. Everything else keeps working, and this refusal arrives only on a submit that names it.

If both are unaccepted you will be told about the service document first. Accepting a resource-scoped document while blocked at the service level changes nothing you can act on.

The body

{
  "type":         "https://siliconcompiler.com/server-errors/terms-not-accepted",
  "title":        "Terms not accepted",
  "status":       403,
  "detail":       "This flow names a PDK covered by an agreement you have not accepted.",
  "instance":     "/v1/jobs/01J9.../submit",

  "terms_scope":  "resource",                     // REQUIRED. "service" | "resource"
  "blocked_by":   "gf22-nda",                     // REQUIRED. Which document
  "decision_url": "https://.../terms/gf22-nda",   // REQUIRED. Where you accept or decline

  "trace_id":     "4bf92f3577b34da6a3ce929d0e0e4736"
}
Member
terms_scopeREQUIRED service or resource — the table above.
blocked_byREQUIRED The document’s id. The same blocked_by that GET /v1/me’s authorized.pdks[] entries carry, so it joins against what you already have.
decision_urlREQUIRED Where a human accepts or declines. This is the member the page exists for.

blocked_by is an opaque key — gf22-nda is a slug somebody typed. Do not parse it. Every document also carries a human title, readable at decision_url and in GET /v1/me.

Where it comes from

POST /v1/jobs/{id}/submit, checked against the manifest re-derived from your uploaded archive — not against what the descriptor claimed.

So a service-scoped block is visible before you ever submit, by reading can_submit on GET /v1/me. A resource-scoped one may not be: it depends on what the real manifest names, and that is not known until the archive is opened.

Getting help with your specific failure

Two members of the body identify your exact request. Quote both when you escalate — with them, whoever helps you finds the one request instead of searching a five-minute window.

  • trace_id — the correlation id. The same value is on the response as a header, so it is in your logs even if the body is not.
  • instance — the request this happened to.

This page documents the error type. It is the same page for everyone who hits it and it cannot see your response — trace_id is what connects the two.

What this is not

entitlement-denied means your account does not hold the resource at all. This means you hold it and have not signed for it.

artifact-not-approved (403) is a per-object decision on a specific output, not a document you can accept. It needs a human approval process, not a click.

Normative definition: the error type registry in the SiliconCompiler v1 API contract. This page is a rendering of it — where the two disagree, the registry is right.
