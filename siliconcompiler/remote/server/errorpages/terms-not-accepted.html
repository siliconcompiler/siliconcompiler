terms-not-accepted

Terms not accepted

403 Forbidden https://siliconcompiler.com/server-errors/terms-not-accepted

There is a terms document you have not accepted, and it covers something this request needs.

This one you can fix yourself. blocked_by names every document in the way, each with where to sign it.

What to do

  1. For each document in blocked_by, open its url in a browser, read it, and sign it.
  2. A document whose entry is {} has no link here: find it by its id in GET /v1/me’s terms, where its title is, and ask the operator where to sign it.
  3. Then retry the original request unchanged.

You may also decline, and you may change your mind later. Declining is a recorded answer, not a failure state.

What each document costs

The documentWhat is blockedHow you will have noticed
a "service" document Everything — no create or submit of any kind. GET /v1/me reports can_submit: false, and blocked_reason is this type’s URI.
an agreement on a resource Only what names or derives from the covered resource. can_submit stays true. This refusal arrives at create for a resource the job names, while staging against the job’s resource set, and on a fetch or a log stream of what derives from it.

For a fetch or a log stream, an acceptance of an earlier version of the document counts.

The body

{
  "type":       "https://siliconcompiler.com/server-errors/terms-not-accepted",
  "title":      "Terms not accepted",
  "status":     403,
  "detail":     "This flow names a PDK covered by an agreement you have not accepted.",
  "instance":   "/v1/jobs",

  "blocked_by": {                                   // REQUIRED
    "gf22-nda":   {"url": "https://.../terms/gf22-nda"},
    "gf22-export": {}                               // no link available
  },

  "trace_id":   "4bf92f3577b34da6a3ce929d0e0e4736"
}
Member
blocked_byREQUIRED Each document in the way, by its terms id, mapped to {"url"}, where a person signs it, or to {} where no link is available. The same shape GET /v1/me’s authorized entries and the artifact listing carry.

A terms id is an opaque key — gf22-nda is a slug somebody typed. Do not parse it. Every document carries a human title in GET /v1/me.

Where it comes from

Every gate. At POST /v1/jobs and at submit, for a service document and for a resource the job names; while the job stages, against what the manifest’s read reports, where it ends the job rejected with this on its error; and at GET /v1/jobs/{id}/artifacts/{artifact_id} and …/logs, for what the artifact or log derives from.

Getting help with your specific failure

Two members of the body identify your exact request. Quote both when you escalate — with them, whoever helps you finds the one request instead of searching a five-minute window.

  • trace_id — the request’s W3C trace id: the one your client’s traceparent carried, where it sent one, so your own logs can find it too.
  • Where this arrived on a job’s error rather than on a request, there is no trace_id: quote the job’s id, the one identifier that finds the run.
  • instance — the request this happened to.

This page documents the error type. It is the same page for everyone who hits it and it cannot see your response — trace_id is what connects the two.

What this is not

entitlement-denied means your account does not hold the resource at all. This means you hold it and have not signed for it.

artifact-not-approved (403) is a per-object decision on a specific output, not a document you can accept. It needs a human approval process, not a click.

Normative definition: the error type registry in the SiliconCompiler v1 API contract. This page is a rendering of it — where the two disagree, the registry is right.
