not-permitted

Not permitted on this job

403 Forbidden https://siliconcompiler.com/server-errors/not-permitted

A write on a job you can read and may not act on: an upload grant, a submit, a cancel or a DELETE on a job you do not own — where, for a cancel or a delete, you also do not hold the owner role in the job’s project. Also a create naming an archived project you are a member of, with detail saying it is archived.

What to do

  1. Stop, and do not offer that action on that job again.
  2. Only the job’s owner may ask for its upload grant or submit it. Only the owner, and a holder of the owner role in its project, may cancel or delete it. Ask one of them.

The body

{
  "type":     "https://siliconcompiler.com/server-errors/not-permitted",
  "title":    "Not permitted on this job",
  "status":   403,
  "detail":   "...",
  "instance": "/v1/jobs/01J9.../cancel",
  "trace_id": "4bf92f3577b34da6a3ce929d0e0e4736"
}

No required extension members. detail is prose and may be reworded at any time.

Getting help with your specific failure

Two members of the body identify your exact request. Quote both when you escalate.

  • trace_id — the request’s W3C trace id: the one your client’s traceparent carried, where it sent one, so your own logs can find it too.
  • instance — the request this happened to.

This page documents the error type. It is the same page for everyone who hits it and it cannot see your response — trace_id is what connects the two.

What this is not

not-found is a job you cannot read at all: a 403 there would confirm it exists. Here you have already read the job, so saying no plainly reveals nothing.

insufficient-scope is the credential lacking the scope for the call. This one is you, with a credential that may make the call, and this job.

Normative definition: the error type registry in the SiliconCompiler v1 API contract. This page is a rendering of it — where the two disagree, the registry is right.
